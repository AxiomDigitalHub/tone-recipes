## Recording context

- **Album:** *The Razors Edge* (1990)
- **Studio:** **Little Mountain Sound Studios**, Vancouver, BC, Canada
- **Producer:** **Bruce Fairbairn**
- **Engineer:** **Mike Fraser**
- Recording dates: April–August 1990

Thunderstruck is THE Razors Edge anthem — opening with Angus' iconic tapped intro on the open B string against fretted notes. The intro is a one-finger-tap technique that sounds far more complex than it is. The tone for the intro is the cleaner side of Angus' Plexi crunch.

- **Strings:** Light .009-.042
- **Notable mods:** Stock — same '68 SG, no mods over decades
- **Era caveat:** Same guitar as 1980 Back in Black. Angus' rig has barely evolved across 50 years.

## Amp + Cab

- **Amp:** **Marshall Super Lead 1959** Plexi (100W, jumpered) — same amp as Back in Black
  - For the tapping intro, settings same as Back in Black but possibly volume ~6 instead of 8 (less saturation, more clarity)
- **Cab:** **Marshall 1960** 4x12 with **Celestion G12-65** speakers
- **Power tubes:** EL34 quad
- **Modifications:** Stock
- **Multi-cab setup:** Single 4x12 for the intro tracking

The Razors Edge era was when Angus was using a Marshall Tube Driver pedal (rare) for some tour applications, but studio sessions remained pedal-free per Mike Fraser interviews.

## Microphones

- **Close mic:** **Shure SM57** close, on-axis
- **Off-axis:** **Sennheiser MD421** off-axis
- **Room mic:** Little Mountain has a moderate live room — possibly a Neumann U87 ~6 feet back. Bruce Fairbairn / Mike Fraser used Compass-Point-style tight close miking with selective room blend

## Technique notes

- **Right-hand attack:** ONE-FINGER tapping technique — Angus taps with his middle finger, pulling off to fretted notes
- **Tapping pattern:** Taps the high B at fret 14, pulls off to fret 7 fretted note, then to open B
- **Volume knob:** Possibly rolled to ~7 for the intro to keep clarity
- **Bend technique:** None in the intro — the tapping is the technique
- **Picking patterns:** No pick on the intro — pure right-hand tap + left-hand fret + pull-off
