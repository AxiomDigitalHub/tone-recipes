## Recording context

- **Album:** *Blood Sugar Sex Magik* (1991)
- **Studio:** **The Mansion** (3110 Laurel Canyon Boulevard, Los Angeles) — a former Houdini residence the band rented and lived in during recording
- **Producer:** **Rick Rubin**
- **Engineer:** Brendan O'Brien
- Recording dates: April–June 1991

The Mansion sessions are part of the album's mythology — the band lived together in the house, tracking parts in different rooms with mobile gear. Under the Bridge was tracked at a quieter time of day, with Frusciante alone on guitar and Anthony Kiedis on vocals; Flea joined for the bass line, and Chad Smith for the drums on a later overdub session.

The song is built on the contrast between the **clean intro/verse** (chimey, almost glassy single-coil tone) and the **chorus crescendo** (overdriven, fuller). The clean verse is the focus of this recipe.

---

## Guitar

- **Model:** **1955 Fender Stratocaster** ("the '55") — Frusciante's primary guitar throughout the BSSM sessions
  - Sunburst finish, stock pickups, maple neck
  - Frusciante bought it from former Buckingham Nicks/Fleetwood Mac member Lindsey Buckingham (some sources contest this)
- **Pickup:** **Position 4** (middle + neck in parallel) for the clean verse — the chimey, hollow tone
- **Tuning:** E standard
- **Strings:** GHS Boomers .010–.046
- **Notable mods:** Stock — vintage 1955 Strat, no modifications. Frusciante is meticulous about original-spec gear.
- **Era caveat:** Frusciante's '55 Strat is the BSSM-era guitar. Different from his post-rehab return guitars (1998+), which include a 1962 Strat and a customized Cort acoustic. The '55 is the Under the Bridge guitar.

**Notes:**
- Under the Bridge's clean verse is famously **NEAR-DRY** — minimal effects. The most-likely effect on the verse take is a touch of CE-1 chorus, but some sources say it's bypassed.
- Frusciante does NOT use overdrive pedals on the verse. The chorus crescendo where the guitar gets bigger is achieved by **playing harder + the amp's natural response**, not pedalboard distortion.
- The chorus crescendo features a wider chord voicing (open D + suspended notes) — that's compositional, not gear.
- Frusciante's BSSM-era pedalboard is otherwise minimalist by Hollywood-rock standards. The era's "minimalist" trend (compared to 80s overproduced rock) is reflected in his rig.

## Amp + Cab

- **Amp #1 (clean verse):** **Marshall JCM 800 2204** (50W) on the **Clean channel** with very low gain — used for the clean intro and verse
  - Settings: Pre-amp 3 (clean), Master 5, Bass 5, Mid 5, Treble 6, Presence 5
- **Amp #2 (chorus crescendo):** Same JCM 800 2204 with Pre-amp pushed higher (~7) for breakup, OR a separate cranked JCM 800 — sources differ
  - Some accounts cite a Marshall Plexi (1959) for the louder sections; Brendan O'Brien's mix notes are inconsistent

For the clean verse focus of this recipe: JCM 800 2204 Clean channel.

- **Cab:** Marshall **1960B** 4x12
  - **Speakers:** Celestion **G12-65** (1980s-spec)
- **Power tubes:** EL34 quad (50W head uses 2 power tubes; 100W heads use 4)
- **Modifications:** Stock
- **Multi-cab setup:** Single 4x12 in the studio

## Microphones

- **Close mic:** **Shure SM57** — close, on-axis, ~1" off the speaker
- **Off-axis:** Sometimes a Beyer M160 ribbon close
- **Room mic:** Yes — The Mansion's natural acoustics added via a Neumann U87 about 6-8 feet back. Brendan O'Brien used room ambience to add space to BSSM tracks

The studio room sound is part of BSSM's character. Under the Bridge feels "intimate" partly because of the close-mic'd guitar + soft room blend.

---

## Technique notes

- **Right-hand attack:** Light pick (Tortex 0.73mm). Frusciante plays with restraint on the verse — the dynamic range comes from picking force variation, not volume knob
- **Picking pattern:** The verse uses a CHORDED arpeggio — fingertip-style strumming with the pick, accenting the bass note. Each chord is held while the right hand articulates inner voicings
- **Slide / glissando:** None on Under the Bridge
- **Vibrato:** Slow hand vibrato applied to held chord shapes
- **Volume knob:** Frusciante rolls back to ~6 for the introspective verses, full for the chorus

## Open questions

- **Is there ANY effect on the verse take?** Some YouTube tone studies suggest a barely-perceptible chorus. Others say it's pure Strat → Marshall → mic.
- **Was the chorus louder section a separate amp or just the same JCM 800 with the gain knob bumped?** The album mix sounds like the same amp, played harder. But Frusciante's live performances often use a dedicated lead amp.
- **What chorus pedal model EXACTLY?** CE-1 vs the smaller Boss CE-2 — multiple sources cite CE-1, others say CE-2.
