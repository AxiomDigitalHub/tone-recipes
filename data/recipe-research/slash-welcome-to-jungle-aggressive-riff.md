## Recording context

- **Album:** *Appetite for Destruction* (1987)
- **Studio:** **Rumbo Recorders**, Canoga Park, CA + overdubs at Take One / Capitol
- **Producer:** **Mike Clink**
- **Engineer:** Mike Clink + Steve Thompson + Michael Barbiero (mix)
- Recording dates: January–April 1987

Welcome to the Jungle is Slash at his most aggressive on the album. Where Sweet Child opens with a clean intro, Welcome to the Jungle is full-throttle from the iconic "AYY-OH!" wah riff onward. Same gear as Sweet Child — different dial-in, different attitude.

---

## Guitar

- **Model:** **Kris Derrig replica '59 Les Paul** — same guitar as Sweet Child. The Derrig is Slash's primary Appetite guitar across every track.
- **Pickup:** **Bridge** Seymour Duncan Alnico II Pro humbucker for the entire song
- **Tuning:** Eb standard (down 1/2 step)
- **Strings:** Ernie Ball Slinky .010–.046
- **Notable mods:** None
- **Era caveat:** See `slash-sweet-child-o-mine-lead.md` — Derrig replica, Alnico II Pro pickups, era-correct for 1987.

## Pedalboard (chronological)

| # | Pedal | Role | Settings (if known) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cry Baby Wah** (Dunlop GCB-95) | The iconic intro filter — "AYY-OH!" pattern | Heel-toe sweep at song tempo | Verified |
| 2 | **Boss DD-2** Digital Delay | Subtle slapback on the solo | Time ~120 ms, Feedback low, Mix 15% | Likely |

**Notes:**
- The wah is THE Welcome to the Jungle pedal. The intro's "AYY-OH" rhythmic filter motif is Slash rocking the wah heel-down to toe-down in time with the kick drum. This is THE signature lick of the song.
- Otherwise, like Sweet Child, no overdrive in front of the amp. The cranked Marshall is the entire gain stage.
- The wah is OFF for the verses and choruses; ON for the intro + solo.

## Amp + Cab

- **Amp:** **Marshall JCM 800 2203** (100W, single-channel) — same S.I.R. rental as Sweet Child
  - Channel: Lead (single-channel master volume)
  - Settings: Pre-amp 8 (HIGHER than Sweet Child's 7 — more aggressive), Master 6, Bass 5, Mid 7, Treble 6, Presence 5
- **Cab:** **Marshall 1960B** 4x12 with **Celestion G12-65** speakers
- **Power tubes:** EL34 quad
- **Modifications:** Stock S.I.R. rental amp
- **Multi-cab setup:** Single 4x12 in studio

The Marshall is dialed slightly more aggressive on Welcome than on Sweet Child — the song demands it. Pre-amp at 8 vs. Sweet Child's 7 = more saturation, more aggression. Same amp; different knob position.

## Microphones

- **Close mic:** **Shure SM57** + **Sennheiser MD421** close, slightly off-axis
- **Off-axis:** Sometimes a Neumann U67 ~3 feet back
- **Room mic:** Mike Clink's tight close-mic'd approach — minimal room blend on Welcome to the Jungle (the album mix is famously dry)

Same mic technique as Sweet Child. Mike Clink's signature close-and-tight Marshall recording.

## Technique notes

- **Right-hand attack:** Heavy pick (Dunlop Tortex 1.14mm), aggressive downstrokes for verse riff, full alternate picking for the solo
- **Wah technique:** Slash's wah is rhythmic — heel-down on the downbeat, toe-up between notes. Locked to the kick drum
- **Pinch harmonics:** Plenty on this song — the squealing accents in the verses are pinch harmonics
- **Bend technique:** Slash's bends are aggressive, often pre-bent and released for vocal effect
- **Volume knob:** Full open throughout — no clean sections to roll back for
