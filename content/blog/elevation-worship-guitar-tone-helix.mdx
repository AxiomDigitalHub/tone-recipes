> **Start Here: The Elevation Tone in Five Blocks:**
> 1. Amp: **Essex A30** (Vox AC30 Top Boost), edge-of-breakup
> 2. Drive: **Minotaur** (Klon-style), low gain for builds and leads
> 3. Modulation: **Chorus** (subtle, optional) for width on clean parts
> 4. Delay: **Vintage Digital** synced to the song (dotted eighth in 4/4, 1/8 in 6/8) + a long delay for ambience
> 5. Reverb: **Searchlights** / **Plateaux**, large, the atmospheric bed

## A Polished, Layered Worship Sound

Elevation Worship's recordings are built from **polished, layered guitar parts** that blend ambient texture with driving rock energy. Rather than one signature voice, the parts split into *roles*: rhythm, lead, and ambient pads, each doing a distinct job. Elevation hasn't published per-song rigs, so this preset aims at the sound rather than anyone's exact gear, starting from the Vox-style chime most modern worship builds on.

On the [Helix](/platforms/helix), you cover all of that in one snapshot-driven preset. This builds it from empty with exact blocks; it ports to Helix Floor, LT, HX Stomp, and Native.

## The Base: A Vox-Style Amp, With Optional Chorus

Start with the **Essex A30** (based on a Vox AC30 Top Boost) at edge-of-breakup: a soft strum stays clean and digging in just starts to grit, with the upper-mid chime of *Joshua Tree*-era Edge parts rather than a Fender's scooped sparkle. The model has no Mid control, as the real top-boost channel doesn't; use Cut, which removes treble as you turn it up, to tame harshness.

A subtle **Chorus** block (low depth, slow rate, modest mix) over the clean amp widens arpeggiated parts. It's optional: we haven't found a published source tying a chorus pedal to a specific Elevation recording, so use it where a part feels narrow and leave it off where it wobbles under the vocal.

| Block | Setting (Helix 0–10 scale) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Essex A30 | Drive 4, Bass 4.5, Treble 6, Cut 5, Presence 4.5, Master 5 | Chimey base |
| Minotaur | Gain 2.5, Treble 5, Output 6 | Builds and leads |
| Chorus | Depth low, Rate slow, Mix ~25% | Optional width |

## Time and Space

- **Vintage Digital** delay — dotted eighth on 4/4 songs (375 ms at 120 BPM), Feedback ~30%, Mix ~20%. Set it as a note value, not milliseconds, and store each song's tempo in its preset.
- **Long delay** — quarter+ note, higher feedback, low mix, for ambient builds.
- **Searchlights** or **Plateaux** reverb — large, Mix ~25%, low cut 120 Hz, high cut 6 kHz.

For an up-tempo song, the reverb is the block to shorten, not the delay; our [Praise tone build](/blog/praise-elevation-worship-guitar-tone-helix) works through the numbers at 127 BPM.

### Elevation songs in 6/8

Not every Elevation song is a 4/4 anthem. *Trust in God* is listed by MultiTracks in C at 74 BPM in 6/8, and MultiTracks counts 6/8 tempos in quarter notes, meaning pairs of eighths. On a song like that, the dotted eighth comes out at 608 ms, one and a half eighth notes, and the repeats fall between the notes of each group of three. Switch the delay to **1/8** (405 ms at 74) for strummed parts and **1/4 dotted** (1,216 ms) for swells. If you tap the sway instead of storing the tempo, the right values become 1/8 triplet and 1/4. Our [Trust in God build](/blog/trust-in-god-elevation-worship-guitar-tone-helix) works through the whole song, and if your band runs Playback, [syncing the Helix to MIDI clock](/blog/helix-midi-clock-playback-click-worship) handles the tempo for you.

## Snapshot Layout

1. **Clean Rhythm** — amp + chorus, delay/reverb low. Sparse verse picking.
2. **Build** — delay mix up, reverb up. Pre-chorus.
3. **Full/Lead** — Minotaur on, both delays, more reverb. Driven choruses and leads.
4. **Ambient** — long delay + big reverb, swell textures.

## On an HX Stomp

Choose between the chorus and the second delay depending on the song — keep Amp · Minotaur · Chorus · Vintage Digital · Reverb for chorus-forward songs, or swap chorus for the long delay for ambient-forward ones. Three snapshots: Clean, Full, Ambient.
