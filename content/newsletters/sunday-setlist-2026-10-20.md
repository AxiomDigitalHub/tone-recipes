# Sunday Setlist — October 25: which tempo is the real one?

**For Sunday October 25, 2026** · draft written 2026-10-20

You got the setlist Tuesday. Here's what to do with it by Sunday. Six songs you're likely to play on October 25: five staples that never leave rotation, plus one climbing the PraiseCharts weekly list right now. Last week the trap was 6/8. This week it's the tempo number itself: half these songs show up on tempo sites at double the speed you actually feel. Build My Life is listed at 140, which would put the dotted eighth at 321 ms, and it sounds wrong the moment the band comes in. The rule is to count where the snare lands and set the delay from that. Same rig as always: AC30-style base (Essex A30 on Helix), Klon-style boost always on, one delay, one plate. Build it once and change one number per song.

## 1. Build My Life — Housefires

Key of G. Tempo apps read it as 140, but the snare lands at 70, so set the dotted eighth to 643 ms with 2 repeats and the mix around 22%. Verses go on snapshot 1 (Clean). The chorus is snapshot 2 (Drive), and only the last tag earns snapshot 3 (Drive+). This one builds slowly, so let it.

## 2. Living Hope — Phil Wickham

Eb, half-time at about 72, which gives you a 625 ms dotted eighth. The record's verses are pad-like and heavily compressed, with an octave-up shimmer on the swells. On Helix that means snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) with the plate up, not more gain. Switch to snapshot 2 (Drive) at the chorus, when the drums open up.

- [Full recipe: Casey Moore's Living Hope tone](https://faderandknob.com/recipe/moore-living-hope)

## 3. King of Kings — Hillsong Worship

Key of D, listed at 136 but felt at 68, so the dotted eighth is 662 ms. The song is a creed told in verses, so stay out of the lyric's way: snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) with volume swells through verses 1 and 2, then snapshot 2 (Drive) from the first chorus. Use snapshot 6 (Ambi Drive) for the final chorus. At this tempo, keep the plate decay under 3 s or the tails start eating the next chord.

- [Hillsong guitar tone on Helix](https://faderandknob.com/blog/hillsong-guitar-tone-helix)
- [Volume swell technique](https://faderandknob.com/blog/volume-swell-technique)

## 4. Battle Belongs — Phil Wickham

Db at 81 (tempo sites say 162, which is the same pulse counted double), so the dotted eighth is 556 ms with the mix at 18%. This is the drive song of the set: snapshot 2 (Drive) from the first chorus, then snapshot 3 (Drive+) for the bridge build. Keep the Klon-style boost at gain 9 o'clock and treble at noon. The lift should come from volume, not more fizz.

- [Phil Wickham guitar tone on Helix](https://faderandknob.com/blog/phil-wickham-guitar-tone-helix)
- [Klon settings: clean boost to full drive](https://faderandknob.com/blog/klon-centaur-settings-guide)

## 5. Praise — Elevation Worship

Ab on the PraiseCharts chart (some charts list A) and a real 127 BPM, so the dotted eighth is 354 ms and you can actually hear it locking to the hi-hat. Use snapshot 2 (Drive) for most of it and snapshot 4 (Lead) for the riff, with the delay mix down to 15% so the riff stays punchy. Fast songs plus a loud room mean harsh guitar, so if front-of-house winces, cut 2 dB at 3 kHz before you touch the amp.

## 6. Jesus Be The Name — Elevation Worship / Tiffany Hudson

This week's riser, top 5 on PraiseCharts' weekly list as of October 20. It's in Db, and it's charted both at 64 and at 128, and both numbers are useful. Use 703 ms (the dotted eighth at 64) on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) for the long open intro, then 352 ms (at 128) on snapshot 2 (Drive) once the full band comes in. The recording runs close to 9 minutes, so plan your snapshot 6 (Ambi Drive) moment for the back half, not the first chorus.
