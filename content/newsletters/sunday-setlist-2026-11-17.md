# Sunday Setlist — November 22: Thanksgiving Sunday, nothing over 78 BPM

**For Sunday November 22, 2026** · draft written 2026-11-17

You got the setlist Tuesday. Here's what to do with it by Sunday. November 22 is Thanksgiving Sunday, and the six picks below come mostly from the top of PraiseCharts' Top 100 Thanksgiving Songs of 2026, plus two perennial staples. It's the slowest set of the year: every song sits between 61 and 78 BPM, so your delay times run long (621 to 738 ms) and the main job is keeping repeats from piling up under slow lyrics. Fewer repeats, lower mix, more reverb. Same rig as always: an AC30-style amp (Essex A30 on Helix), a Klon-style boost that stays on, one delay, one plate.

## 1. God I'm Just Grateful — Elevation Worship / Chandler Moore

Top of PraiseCharts' Thanksgiving list this year. MultiTracks lists the live master in D at 72 BPM, so the dotted eighth is 625 ms (2 repeats, mix 18%). This one builds from a near-whisper, so start the verses on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) with swells rather than strumming. Step to snapshot 2 (Drive) when the band comes in for the chorus and save snapshot 3 (Drive+) for the final repeated tag, where the room is loudest.

## 2. 10,000 Reasons (Bless the Lord) — Matt Redman

A perennial staple and #6 on PraiseCharts' Thanksgiving list. The original master is in G at 72.5 BPM, so the dotted eighth is 621 ms, which is 4 ms off God I'm Just Grateful. One tap covers both songs. It's a strummed song, so play the verses on snapshot 1 (Clean) and the choruses on snapshot 2 (Drive) with the Klon-style gain around 10 o'clock. Keep the delay mix at 15% so the repeats don't smear the strum pattern.

- [Vox AC30 settings guide](https://faderandknob.com/blog/vox-ac30-settings-guide)
- [Worship guitar tone on Helix](https://faderandknob.com/blog/worship-guitar-tone-helix)

## 3. Gratitude — Brandon Lake

The one exception to the dotted-eighth rule this week. MultiTracks lists the House of Miracles master in B at 78 BPM in 6/8, so the eighth note is 385 ms and the felt beat is 1,154 ms. Store the tempo at 78 and set the delay to 1/8 for the choruses and 1/4 dotted for verse swells. If you tap the felt beat instead, the Helix reads about 52 BPM, so switch the note value to 1/8 triplet. The verses can take 3.5–4 s of plate decay on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi).

- [Gratitude guitar tone on Helix](https://faderandknob.com/blog/gratitude-brandon-lake-guitar-tone-helix)

## 4. King of Kings — Hillsong Worship

#5 on the Thanksgiving list and a perennial staple. The Awake master is in D at 68 BPM, so the dotted eighth is 662 ms (2 repeats, mix 20%). The verses walk through a story, so keep them on snapshot 1 (Clean) and let the vocal lead. Move to snapshot 6 (Ambi Drive) for the bridge build, then snapshot 3 (Drive+) for the last chorus.

- [Hillsong guitar tone on Helix](https://faderandknob.com/blog/hillsong-guitar-tone-helix)

## 5. Thank You Jesus for the Blood — Charity Gayle

#4 on PraiseCharts' Thanksgiving list. MultiTracks lists the Endless Praise original in Bb at 61 BPM, the slowest song in the set, so the dotted eighth is 738 ms. At that length every repeat lands on the next word, so use 1 repeat with the mix at 12%, or turn on ducking so the repeats only bloom in the gaps. Play almost the whole song on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) with swells. Use snapshot 6 (Ambi Drive) only for the last chorus.

- [Ducking delay: repeats that get out of the way](https://faderandknob.com/blog/ducking-delay-sidechain-repeats)
- [Volume swell technique](https://faderandknob.com/blog/volume-swell-technique)

## 6. Goodness of God — Bethel Music / Jenn Johnson

#2 on the Thanksgiving list, back from last week as promised. Same coordinates: Ab at 63 BPM, dotted eighth 714 ms, 1–2 repeats at 15% mix. Verses on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi), choruses on snapshot 2 (Drive), and snapshot 6 (Ambi Drive) for the build into the final chorus.
