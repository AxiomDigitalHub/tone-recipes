# Sunday Setlist — November 29: Advent starts, delay from 304 to 662 ms

**For Sunday November 29, 2026** · draft written 2026-11-24

You got the setlist Tuesday. Here's what to do with it by Sunday. November 29 is the first Sunday of Advent, and four of the six picks below come from the top of PraiseCharts' Top 100 Christmas Worship Songs of 2026 and its Top Songs for Advent list. The other two are perennial staples that PraiseCharts also lists among modern songs that work at Christmas. Last week nothing was over 78 BPM. This week the set runs from 68 to 148 BPM, so the dotted eighth moves from 662 ms down to 304 ms, and the fast songs need different delay settings from the slow ones, not only a different time. Same rig as always: an AC30-style amp (Essex A30 on Helix), a Klon-style boost that stays on, one delay, one plate.

## 1. Angels (Glory to God) — Phil Wickham

Near the top of PraiseCharts' Christmas list this year. MultiTracks lists the original master in A at 118 BPM, so the dotted eighth is 381 ms (3 repeats, mix 22%). At this tempo the delay can carry a picked eighth-note line, so play the verses on snapshot 1 (Clean) with single notes and let the repeats fill the gaps. Go to snapshot 2 (Drive) for the choruses and snapshot 3 (Drive+) for the last one. Pull the plate decay back to about 2 s so the tail clears before the next bar.

## 2. Holy Forever (Christmas) — Chris Tomlin

Also near the top of the Christmas list. MultiTracks and Worship Together both list the Christmas recording in B at 72 BPM. The standard version is in Db at the same 72 BPM, so the dotted eighth is still 625 ms and only the key has moved. If you saved a Holy Forever preset this autumn, reuse it as is (2 repeats, mix 20%). Verses on snapshot 1 (Clean), snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) for the 'holy, holy, holy' lines, and snapshot 3 (Drive+) once the full band lands.

- [Holy Forever guitar tone on Helix](https://faderandknob.com/blog/holy-forever-guitar-tone-helix)

## 3. Joy to the World (Joyful, Joyful) — Phil Wickham

On both the Christmas list and the Advent list, and the fastest song in the set. MultiTracks lists the original master in Db at 148 BPM, so the dotted eighth is 304 ms. At that length the repeats sit close behind every strum, so drop the mix to 12% with 2 repeats, or switch the delay to a straight quarter note (405 ms) if the dotted pattern fights the drummer. Play it on snapshot 2 (Drive) from the first verse, with the Klon-style gain around 11 o'clock, and move to snapshot 3 (Drive+) for the 'Joyful, joyful' section.

## 4. O Come All Ye Faithful (His Name Shall Be) — Passion / Melodie Malone

First on PraiseCharts' Top Songs for Advent list. MultiTracks lists the original master in Eb at 76 BPM, so the dotted eighth is 592 ms (2 repeats, mix 18%). The congregation knows the carol, so keep the verses on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) with swells and leave the melody to the voices. Move to snapshot 6 (Ambi Drive) for the 'His name shall be' section and snapshot 3 (Drive+) for the last chorus.

## 5. What a Beautiful Name — Hillsong Worship

A perennial staple that PraiseCharts lists among modern songs that work at Christmas. D at 68 BPM, so the dotted eighth is 662 ms (2 repeats, mix 20%). Play the verses on snapshot 1 (Clean) and the chorus on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi). Save snapshot 3 (Drive+) for the bridge ('Death could not hold You'), which is the only real peak in the song.

- [Full recipe: Hendroff's What a Beautiful Name tone](https://faderandknob.com/recipe/hendroff-what-a-beautiful-name)
- [Hillsong guitar tone on Helix](https://faderandknob.com/blog/hillsong-guitar-tone-helix)

## 6. Living Hope — Phil Wickham

The other perennial staple on that list. Eb at 72 BPM, so the dotted eighth is 625 ms, the same delay time as Holy Forever (Christmas). One tap covers both songs (2 repeats, mix 22%). Play the verses on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) with swells. At 'Hallelujah, praise the One who set me free', move to snapshot 6 (Ambi Drive) rather than snapshot 3 (Drive+), so the gain comes up while the space stays.
