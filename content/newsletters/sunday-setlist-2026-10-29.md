# Sunday Setlist — November 1: four songs at 72, one in 6/8

**For Sunday November 1, 2026** · draft written 2026-10-29

You got the setlist Tuesday. Here's what to do with it by Sunday. November 1 is All Saints' Day, and it lands on a Sunday this year, so expect older hymn-rooted songs next to the current chart. Five picks: four staples that never leave rotation, plus one that's been climbing PraiseCharts all year. This week's pattern is easy to miss: four of the five sit within a couple of BPM of 72, so your dotted eighth barely moves (621 to 629 ms) and you can leave it alone between songs. The one that breaks the pattern is in 6/8, and that's the one to rehearse. Same rig as always: AC30-style base (Essex A30 on Helix), Klon-style boost always on, one delay, one plate.

## 1. Holy Forever — Chris Tomlin

Db at 72, so the dotted eighth is 625 ms with 2 repeats and the mix around 20%. Top 3 on PraiseCharts' weekly list as of October 29. Verses sit on snapshot 1 (Clean). Hold snapshot 2 (Drive) for the chorus and save snapshot 3 (Drive+) for the bridge, which is where this song actually peaks.

## 2. Goodness of God — Bethel Music / Jenn Johnson

Ab at 63 on the Victory recording, the slowest song in the set, so the dotted eighth stretches to 714 ms. Drop the mix to 15% so the repeats don't blur the lyric. Play verses on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) with swells, switch to snapshot 2 (Drive) at the first chorus, and use snapshot 6 (Ambi Drive) for the final build.

- [Full recipe: Goodness of God ambient tone](https://faderandknob.com/recipe/hislop-goodness-of-god)
- [Bethel Music guitar tone on Helix](https://faderandknob.com/blog/bethel-music-guitar-tone-helix)

## 3. 10,000 Reasons (Bless the Lord) — Matt Redman

Key of G at about 72.5, so the dotted eighth is 621 ms. Most of this song is clean: snapshot 1 (Clean) all the way through, with snapshot 2 (Drive) only on the last two choruses. The original recording drifts in tempo, so if your drummer isn't on a click, tap the delay in at the top instead of trusting a preset value.

- [Volume swell technique](https://faderandknob.com/blog/volume-swell-technique)
- [Vox AC30 settings: jangle to crunch](https://faderandknob.com/blog/vox-ac30-settings-guide)

## 4. Cornerstone — Hillsong Worship

Key of C at 71.5, so the dotted eighth is 629 ms. The 7-minute live version is built on a piano and a hymn melody, so the guitar's job is to stay out of the way. Use snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) in the verses and stay an octave above the keys pad. Snapshot 2 (Drive) comes in on the chorus, and the plate decay should stay under 3 s.

- [Ambient guitar vs. the keys pad: who gets which octave](https://faderandknob.com/blog/ambient-guitar-and-keys-pad-who-gets-which-octave)
- [Hillsong guitar tone on Helix](https://faderandknob.com/blog/hillsong-guitar-tone-helix)

## 5. I Know A Name — Elevation Worship / Brandon Lake / Chris Brown

This week's riser: top 5 on PraiseCharts' 365-day list as of October 28. It's in C and in 6/8. MultiTracks lists it at 86 BPM and other sites at 172 (the eighth-note count). Skip the dotted eighth, because 523 ms fights the triple grouping. Use the straight eighth instead, 30000÷86 = 349 ms, with the mix at 15%. Play snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) for the verses, snapshot 2 (Drive) for the chorus, and snapshot 3 (Drive+) for the bridge build.
