# Sunday Setlist — August 9: 6 songs, one AC30

**Drafted:** 2026-08-04 · **Target Sunday:** August 9, 2026

You got the setlist. Now you need the tones. Six songs churches are most likely to sing this Sunday — four perennial staples and two that have climbed fast — each with the delay time, the snapshot, and the one move that actually makes the part work. Everything below runs off one AC30-style base (Essex A30 on Helix), a Klon-style boost always on, a dotted-eighth delay, and a plate. Build the preset once; change four numbers per song.

---

### Goodness of God
*Bethel Music*

82 BPM in Ab — dotted-eighth delay at 549 ms, two repeats, mix around 25%. Verses live on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) with the volume pedal heel-down: pluck the chord, swell in, let the plate do the attack. The final chorus is the only place you need snapshot 3 (Drive+) — the JHS-into-AC30 push, not a lead tone.

### Holy Forever
*Chris Tomlin*

72 BPM in Db — dotted-eighth at 625 ms. The whole song is a build, so let the snapshots do it: snapshot 1 (Clean) for verse 1, snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) with reverb up for the "holy, holy, holy" pre-chorus, snapshot 3 (Drive+) once the full band lands. Resist adding gain before the chorus — the lift is reverb mix and the boost, not distortion.

### Great Are You Lord
*All Sons & Daughters*

~72 BPM in A, and it's in 6/8 — which is where most worship players get this wrong. A dotted-eighth delay in 6/8 lands on the beat and turns to mud, so switch to a straight eighth at 278 ms with the mix pulled back to 12%. This is a Telecaster-and-amp tone: snapshot 1 (Clean) the whole way, boost always on, and let the AC30 bloom carry it.

- [Full recipe: Stu G's Great Are You Lord tone](/recipe/garrard-great-are-you-lord)

### Way Maker
*Leeland*

68 BPM in E — dotted-eighth at 662 ms, the longest delay on this list, so keep repeats at two or the verses smear. The volume pedal is the song: snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi), heel-down, swell every chord change with no pick attack at all. Snapshot 4 (Lead) only for the chorus line, where the Klon-style boost drives the AC30 into a singing mid-forward tone.

- [Full recipe: Casey Moore's Way Maker tone](/recipe/moore-way-maker)
- [Way Maker guitar tone on Helix](/blog/way-maker-guitar-tone-helix)

### WASHED
*ELEVATION RHYTHM*

139 BPM — dotted-eighth at 324 ms, and this is the one where you have to cut the reverb. A long plate at 139 BPM is soup: drop decay to 1.8 s, predelay 20 ms, mix to 15%. Snapshot 2 (Drive) carries the driving eighths through the verse, snapshot 6 (Ambi Drive) for the chorus so you keep the grit without losing the wash.

- [Elevation Worship guitar tone on Helix](/blog/elevation-worship-guitar-tone-helix)
- [Why your delay sounds muddy](/blog/why-delay-sounds-muddy)

### Who Else
*Gateway Worship*

Charted at 136 BPM in Ab but felt half-time at 68, so you get two correct delay times: 331 ms if you're playing the driving eighths, 662 ms if you're floating pads over the half-time feel. Pick per section — 331 ms on snapshot 2 (Drive) for the choruses, 662 ms on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) for the verses. Same preset, one delay-time change, and the part suddenly sits.
