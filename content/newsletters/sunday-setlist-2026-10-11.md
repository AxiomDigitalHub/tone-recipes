# Sunday Setlist — October 18: half the set is in 6/8

**For Sunday October 18, 2026** · drafted 2026-10-11

You got the setlist Tuesday. Here's what to do with it by Sunday. Six songs the charts say you're most likely to play on October 18: four that have sat in the CCLI top 20 all year, plus two climbing right now — both of them gratitude songs, which is a ramp that starts about now and runs through November. One thing worth knowing before you build: three of the six are in 6/8, where a dotted-eighth delay quietly ruins everything. It all runs off one AC30-style base (Essex A30 on Helix), a Klon-style boost always on, one delay block and one plate. Build it once, then change two numbers per song.

---

## 1. What a Beautiful Name — Hillsong Worship

68 BPM in D — dotted-eighth at 662 ms, two repeats, mix around 22%. Verses sit on snapshot 1 (Clean) with the boost already on; the title-line lift is snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) with the plate up, not more gain. Save snapshot 3 (Drive+) for the final chorus — the song peaks once, and if you spend it in verse 2 you have nowhere left to go.

## 2. Gratitude — Brandon Lake

78 BPM in B, and it's 6/8 — so kill the dotted eighth. At 45000÷78 it lands on 577 ms and fights the compound-meter grouping until the part smears; use the straight eighth instead, 30000÷78 = 385 ms, with mix back at 12%. Snapshot 1 (Clean) carries the verses on the AC30's bloom alone, and the stripped bridge wants you off the strings entirely.

- [Why the dotted eighth breaks in 6/8](https://faderandknob.com/blog/dotted-eighth-delay-no-tap-tempo)
- [Delay time BPM calculator](https://faderandknob.com/blog/delay-time-bpm-calculator)

## 3. Trust In God — Elevation Worship

Charted at 148 BPM in C and felt in 6/8, which gives you two usable eighth-note times: 203 ms against the driving subdivision, 405 ms against the half-time 74 feel. Pick per section — 203 ms on snapshot 2 (Drive) for the choruses, 405 ms on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) for the verses. Same preset, one delay-time change, and the part stops arguing with the drummer.

- [Elevation Worship guitar tone on Helix](https://faderandknob.com/blog/elevation-worship-guitar-tone-helix)
- [Sunday-morning snapshot layout](https://faderandknob.com/blog/helix-worship-snapshots-sunday-morning)

## 4. Firm Foundation (He Won't) — Cody Carnes

The studio cut is 75 BPM in Db and live versions run it at 150 — either way it's 6/8, so straight eighths again: 200 ms at the fast count, 400 ms at the slow one. The trap here is reverb, not delay — a long plate under a 150 BPM 6/8 turns the chorus to mush, so pull decay back to 1.6 s and predelay to 20 ms. Snapshot 2 (Drive) through the verses, snapshot 6 (Ambi Drive) once the room is singing.

- [When reverb tails collide with the tempo](https://faderandknob.com/blog/reverb-decay-vs-song-tempo-when-tails-collide)

## 5. God I'm Just Grateful — Elevation Worship / Chandler Moore

72 BPM in D and properly 4/4, so you finally get a clean dotted eighth: 625 ms, three repeats, mix 25%. This is a volume-pedal song — snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi), heel down, swell into each chord change with no pick attack at all. Snapshot 4 (Lead) only for the turnaround.

- [Volume swell technique](https://faderandknob.com/blog/volume-swell-technique)
- [Sunday-morning snapshot layout](https://faderandknob.com/blog/helix-worship-snapshots-sunday-morning)

## 6. In The Presence Of The King — Charity Gayle

73 BPM in G — dotted eighth at 616 ms, two repeats, mix 20%. New enough that most rooms are still learning it, so keep the build obvious: snapshot 1 (Clean) through the verses, snapshot 3 (Drive+) from the chorus, boost on the whole way. If it's fighting the keys, that's 2 dB out at 2.5 kHz, not a different amp.
