# Sunday Setlist — November 15: 352 ms to 714 ms, one preset

**For Sunday November 15, 2026** · draft written 2026-11-10

You got the setlist Tuesday. Here's what to do with it by Sunday. Five picks for November 15, the week before Thanksgiving Sunday: four perennial staples plus one song climbing the Thanksgiving lists. The set covers a wide tempo range, from Goodness of God at 63 BPM to Thank God I'm Free at 128, so your dotted eighth runs from 714 ms down to 352 ms. Tap tempo every song this week, and drop the delay mix as the tempo goes up. Same rig as always: an AC30-style amp (Essex A30 on Helix), a Klon-style boost that stays on, one delay, one plate.

## 1. Thank God I'm Free — ELEVATION RHYTHM

This week's riser: #3 on PraiseCharts' Top 100 Thanksgiving Songs of 2026. MultiTracks lists the original master in E at 128 BPM, so the dotted eighth is 352 ms. That's short enough to blur into the rhythm part, so use 1 repeat with the mix at 12% and pull the plate decay down to about 1.5 s. Play the whole song on snapshot 2 (Drive). Click to snapshot 3 (Drive+) only for the last chorus, because at 128 more gain just adds noise.

## 2. House of the Lord — Phil Wickham

A perennial staple. The Hymn of Heaven original is in Bb at 86 BPM, so the dotted eighth is 523 ms (2 repeats, mix 18%). This is a strumming song, so the boost does the work: set the Klon-style gain around 10 o'clock and use snapshot 2 (Drive) for every chorus. Switch to snapshot 4 (Lead) only for the instrumental hook between sections. If your band plays the acoustic arrangement in G at 68, the dotted eighth is 662 ms, the same as What a Beautiful Name below.

## 3. Living Hope — Phil Wickham

A perennial staple: Eb at 72, so the dotted eighth is 625 ms (2 repeats, mix 22%). The verses are pad territory, so play them on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) with swells and keep your pick attack out of it. The 'Hallelujah, praise the One who set me free' section is where the song turns. Move to snapshot 6 (Ambi Drive) there, not snapshot 3 (Drive+), so the gain comes up while the space stays.

## 4. What a Beautiful Name — Hillsong Worship

A perennial staple: D at 68 BPM, so the dotted eighth is 662 ms (2 repeats, mix 20%). If you're playing House of the Lord's acoustic version too, that's the same delay time, so one tap covers both songs. Play the verses on snapshot 1 (Clean) and the chorus on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi). Save snapshot 3 (Drive+) for the bridge ('Death could not hold You'), which is the only real peak in the song.

- [Full recipe: Nigel Hendroff's What a Beautiful Name tone](https://faderandknob.com/recipe/hendroff-what-a-beautiful-name)

## 5. Goodness of God — Bethel Music / Jenn Johnson

#2 on PraiseCharts' Thanksgiving list, and it'll be back next week too. The Victory recording is in Ab at 63, the slowest song in the set, so the dotted eighth stretches to 714 ms. At that length a second repeat lands on the next lyric, so keep it to 1–2 repeats with the mix at 15%. Play the verses on snapshot 5 (Clean Ambi) and the choruses on snapshot 2 (Drive). Use snapshot 6 (Ambi Drive) for the 'all my life You have been faithful' build.
