Fender opened preorders on November 24 for the Godzilla Telecaster Black Paisley, a Made in Japan limited-run model built for the Telecaster's 75th anniversary. It follows the Godzilla Stratocaster from October 2025. Guitar World, Gearnews and MusicRadar all carried it between November 24 and 25.

The graphics will get the attention. The part that affects how the guitar is used is electrical: two high-output humbuckers and a distortion circuit built into the guitar.

## What's Confirmed

- **Body:** alder, no pickguard, Black Paisley finish with Godzilla artwork by Tom Neely
- **Neck:** maple, Modern C profile, bolt-on
- **Fingerboard:** "Obsidian Ebony", described as maple infused with black resin, 9.5-inch radius, 22 medium jumbo frets, skyline and Godzilla inlays
- **Pickups:** two custom Godzilla humbuckers, the same model used in the 2025 Godzilla Strat
- **Switching:** three-way selector
- **Distortion:** built-in circuit engaged from the master volume pot
- **Case:** matching Godzilla-branded hard case
- **Price:** $2,500 per Guitar World and Gearnews, $2,499 per MusicRadar. £2,199 in the UK
- **Availability:** direct from Fender. The official Japan launch is December 4

MusicRadar reports that international orders are expected to ship in July 2027. The other two outlets give no ship date. Fender calls the model a limited run and has not published a quantity.

There is also a Japan-only Masterbuilt version by Andy Hicks with UV-reactive artwork and a single hand-wound Josefina Campos Blackguard bridge pickup. Gearnews lists it at ¥5,500,000; MusicRadar says price and date are still to be confirmed. That one is a different instrument and has no distortion circuit mentioned.

## What Isn't Stated

Three things the coverage does not give, all of which matter for setting it up:

1. **The circuit type.** It is called a distortion and compared to Godzilla's roar. Nobody says whether it is op-amp, transistor, diode-clipped, or what its output level is.
2. **Power.** An active circuit needs a battery or another supply. None of the three articles mentions one.
3. **Pickup output.** "High-output" has no figure attached. No DC resistance, no magnet type.

The switch itself is described two ways: push/push by Guitar World and Gearnews, push/pull by MusicRadar. Either way it is on the master volume.

## What an Onboard Distortion Does to the Chain

A distortion inside the guitar is first in the signal path, ahead of everything, with no way to move it. Four consequences follow.

**It sits before the volume control or after it, and that decides everything.** If the circuit comes after the volume pot, rolling the volume down cleans it up like a pedal would. If it comes before, the volume only makes the distorted signal quieter. The published specs don't say which. Check this first when demos appear.

**A wah, compressor or fuzz can never go in front of it.** Wah after distortion is a harsher, more filter-like sound than wah before. If your board depends on that order, this guitar overrides it whenever the circuit is on.

**The amp should be set cleaner than usual.** Two clipping stages in series multiply. Running the onboard circuit into an already saturated amp gives more noise and compression and less note definition. Set the amp for a clean or edge-of-breakup sound with the circuit off, then engage it. The principle is the same as for any distortion pedal into an amp, covered in [overdrive vs distortion vs fuzz](/blog/overdrive-vs-distortion-vs-fuzz).

**The noise gate goes after it, not before.** With a pedal you can gate the guitar signal ahead of the gain. Here the first place a gate can go is after the circuit, so it has to work harder.

## Setting an Amp or Modeler Around It

Starting points, not measurements of this guitar. With the onboard circuit engaged:

- **Amp gain:** low. On a Helix clean-platform amp, Drive 2.5 to 3.5 on the 0–10 scale
- **Bass:** 4.0 to 4.5. High-output humbuckers already load the low end, and distortion before the amp adds more
- **Mid:** 5.5 to 6.0
- **Treble and Presence:** 5.0, then reduce Presence if the top end gets abrasive
- **Input:** on Helix or HX Stomp, turn the input pad on if the first block clips, since the circuit's output level is unknown
- **Gate:** Helix input gate threshold around -48 dB as a first setting, tighter if needed

With the circuit off, treat it as a two-humbucker guitar and bring the amp gain back up. For pedal gain in front of humbuckers, [overdrive with humbuckers](/blog/overdrive-with-humbuckers-settings) has the adjustments.

If you want to hear the signal-chain behavior before spending $2,500, put a distortion block first in a Helix preset, ahead of everything including the wah, and leave it there. Vermin Dist or Deez One Vintage at Gain 5.0 will do. The voicing will not match, because the voicing of Fender's circuit has not been described, but the constraints are the same.

## Assessment

At $2,500 with a possible seven-month wait, this is a collector's guitar, and it is priced as one. The useful question for a player is whether the distortion circuit is a tool or a decoration, and the answer depends on the three unstated items above. Until Fender or a reviewer publishes where the circuit sits relative to the volume pot and how it is powered, that question stays open.
