# Round 1: Marketing Lead opening position (Mara Quinn)

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Owns:** reach that converts into site visits and email subscribers.
**Verdict:** the current routine isn't a weak social strategy. It's an RSS feed rendered as Instagram posts, and Instagram treats it accordingly. Kill it in its current form this week.

## 1. Diagnosis: why 7 posts earned reach 1

1. **Nothing to distribute it to.** With 0 followers and no profile picture, there's no seed audience. A static 16:9 feed photo barely enters non-follower testing, which in 2026 mostly runs through Reels and carousels judged on early sends, saves and watch time. Seven posts, zero interactions: the trial pool never formed.
2. **The account looks like a bot.** No avatar, an identical caption skeleton every day, the same blog post retried three times (published_posts.txt shows 3 `failed` attempts on "Jumping Your Amp's Channels" and then a duplicate publish 13 minutes later), and 12–13 hashtags on every post against the ~5-hashtag cap reported since Dec 2025. Each of those alone is a soft spam signal. Together they probably get the account classified as low-trust.
3. **The value sits behind the click, and the platform penalizes that.** The posts are a headline plus a teaser plus "link in bio." Nobody saves or sends a teaser. The settings, the thing people actually want, are never in the post.
4. **The image does no work.** A generic AI studio gear shot at 16:9 shows up as a thin letterbox in a 4:5 feed. It has no text, no knob values and no song, so it says nothing in the 2 seconds we get.
5. **Wrong topics, wrong vocabulary.** The picker is "newest blog post," which gave us speaker magnet weights, Y-cable impedance and DSP-unit arithmetic. Those are deep how-to and theory posts, the format that draws 4.8 users/page, while settings guides draw 17.2. The captions fail the escalator test on every line ("DSP units," "Dual Cab block," "gain stages," "signal chain"). And the ICP, worship players on Helix/HX Stomp, got about one relevant post out of seven.
6. **No destination.** "Link in bio" leads to a homepage, with no post-matched landing, no UTM and no email hook. Even if it worked, we couldn't measure it.

## 2. Reach goal and targets

**North-star:** email subscribers sourced from social (UTM-tagged signups). Followers are a means, not the goal.

| | Day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| IG median reach per post | 300 | 1,000 | 2,500 |
| IG followers | 150 | 500 | 1,200 |
| Pinterest monthly outbound clicks | 50 | 250 | 700 |
| Social-sourced site sessions / mo (all channels) | 150 | 600 | 1,500 |
| Social-sourced email subscribers / mo | 10 | 40 | 100 |

These are commitments for a zero-follower niche account running one excellent format, not stretch goals. C2's unwrapping window (Dec 25–Jan 15) falls around day 60–80, which is our best shot at beating them.

**Leading indicators, ranked:**
1. **Sends per reach** (DM shares): the strongest documented non-follower signal, and the truest sign we made something worth passing to the bandmate.
2. **Saves per reach**: proof the post is reference material ("I'll set this up Saturday").
3. **Profile visits per reach → follow rate**: tells us whether the grid converts curiosity.
4. **Link-in-bio / Pinterest outbound clicks**: the bridge to the north-star.
5. **Follows**: lagging, reported but never optimized directly.

Likes and views are logged and ignored. **Kill rule:** if median non-follower reach on carousels is under 150 by day 45, the format is wrong. Change the format, not the cadence.

## 3. Channel strategy

| Channel | Role | Automation | Decision |

| **YouTube Shorts** | The real prize for this audience, but only with real audio | Blocked | **Hire the audio contractor.** That's the decision that unlocks Shorts and Reels |
| **Facebook worship/Helix groups** | Where worship volunteers actually are | Human only, Nathan Cross rules | Keep human; no Page autopost (≈ zero reach) |
| X, TikTok queue, blog-hero posts | n/a | n/a | **Stop.** Remove the TikTok/YT queue-forever path |

**Human time:** one 60-minute setup (avatar, bio with AI disclosure, UTM link-in-bio page with an email hook, 3 Highlights), then the existing 20-minute Monday window. My one ask beyond that is **10 human minutes per week replying to comments and DMs.** A zero-follower account with nobody home stays at zero.

## 4. Positioning in the feed

**Famous for: "the account that tells you exactly where to set every knob."** Every post is a screenshot-able answer that's useful without clicking.

Escalator-passing hooks, plain language only:
- "The songs come Tuesday. Here's the Sunday tone." (worship, album-level credit)
- "Every knob on a Deluxe Reverb has a right answer."
- "You bought the HX Stomp. Here's where the good sounds are." (C2 unwrapping)
- "Real numbers, not 'add some warmth.'"

Content mix for the first 90 days: 50% worship songs on Helix/HX Stomp, 30% amp/pedal settings guides (the 17.2 users/page format), 20% seasonal (gift/unwrapping). Zero speaker-physics posts as first touch.

## 5. Non-negotiables and risks

- **Data-derived values only.** Every number on a card comes from the recipe/settings record and is never generated in the caption. A wrong gain value is the one trust break we can't recover from.
- **No synthetic guitar audio, ever.** Silent or music-only motion is fine. A fake riff kills us with this audience.
- **AI disclosure:** "AI-researched, corrections published" in the bio, Meta's AI label on generated imagery, labeled personas. Never imply a human tested a tone.
- **No fake community:** no engagement pods, no follow/unfollow, no automated comments or DMs, no purchased anything, no volume-count flexing.
- **Worship attribution:** album-level credits; no album art (copyright). Artist and song names in text only.
- **Account safety:** max 1 feed post/day plus 1 Trial Reel; idempotent publish (never retry-publish the same item); ≤5 hashtags; caption variety; no identical simultaneous cross-posts.
