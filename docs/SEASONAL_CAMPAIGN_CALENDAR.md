# Seasonal Acquisition Campaign Calendar

**Created:** 2026-07-25
**Companion to:** `MESSAGING_FRAMEWORK.md` (pillars, segments) and `DISTRIBUTION_AUTOMATION_OS.md` (how each campaign runs itself).
**Premise:** Guitar gear buying is intensely seasonal (gifting, tax refunds, Black Friday, NAMM, church calendar). We sell digital recipes and subscriptions, not gear, so our seasonal play is different from a retailer's and better: we win **before** the purchase (buying guides), **at** the purchase (gift guides and deals coverage), and most importantly **after** the purchase (the "I just got this, now what?" moment, which is exactly when someone needs a recipe). Nobody else in the niche owns the post-gift moment. It is pillar P4 with a calendar attached.

**The three-layer play for every season:**
1. **Rank layer (T-8 weeks):** publish/refresh the seasonal SEO content so Google and Bing have it indexed before intent spikes. AI assistants pick pages up faster, but Google needs the runway.
2. **Capture layer (T-2 weeks to day-of):** newsletter sends, social posts, on-site banners pointing at the seasonal hub; deal coverage in the news pipeline.
3. **Convert layer (day-of to T+4 weeks):** post-purchase onboarding content, first-run email sequences, and the subscription offer while the new gear is still exciting.

## Campaign specs

### C1: New Volunteer Wave (September 2026): first campaign out the gate

- **Audience/segment:** worship guitarists, new volunteers; Team-tier prospects (worship leaders).
- **Rank layer (launch by Aug 10):** cluster around the existing "New to the Worship Team? First-Month Guide": "first worship set on HX Stomp", "5 Helix presets every new worship guitarist needs", "the volunteer guitarist's gear checklist". All link to the Worship Set Pack and the /worship hub.
- **Capture:** newsletter feature; Nathan Cross answers in r/worshipguitar and r/worshipleaders when September onboarding threads appear (human-posted per outreach rules).
- **Convert:** Set Pack $19; email sequence "four Sundays, four skills".
- **Automation:** content and newsletter fully automated; Reddit stays human.
- **KPI:** worship cluster impressions (GSC), Set Pack sales, /worship email signups.
- **Note:** the worship cluster is still authority-gated in Google (4 of 5 posts unindexed as of Jul 21). The backlink circuit in `DISTRIBUTION_AUTOMATION_OS.md` is a hard dependency; Bing/DDG/AI assistants will carry the cluster in the meantime.

### C2: Holiday Gift Engine (October 2026 - January 2027): the big one

**Phase A, rank (publish by Oct 15):**
- "Gift Guide for Guitarists 2026" hub written for the *non-player buyer* (spouse, parent, kid): budget tiers, "safe bets", what not to buy. Almost nobody writes for the buyer who doesn't play; low competition, high link-worthiness.
- Refresh every "Best X in 2026" page with current prices and a "holiday pick" callout (redate per playbook rules, no near-duplicates).
- "Gifts under $50/$100/$200 a guitarist will actually use" (pedals we already have settings guides for; every item links to its recipe: differentiation).

**Phase B, deals (Nov 20 - Dec 2):**
- News pipeline runs Black Friday modeler/pedal deal coverage (Tier 3 news content per news-strategy; Reverb data + brand announcements). Update cadence daily during the window; this is freshness the index rewards.
- **Our own offer:** annual-plan promo (e.g. first year of Pass at a promo price, or Pass + one Set Pack bundle). Requires only a Stripe coupon once checkout is live. One newsletter send + site banner. No fake urgency; one honest window, clearly dated.

**Phase C, gifting digital (Dec 10-24):**
- Gift a Pass: Stripe supports gift-style one-time purchases; minimum viable version is a "gift card PDF" the buyer prints (code redeemed at signup). Last-minute digital gifts peak Dec 20-24 when shipping cutoffs pass and we are one of the few things still deliverable.

**Phase D, the unwrapping window (Dec 25 - Jan 15), highest priority of the whole calendar:**
- Publish by Dec 18: "Just got a Boss Katana? The 7 settings to try first" (adapt the existing Katana deep-dive), same for HX Stomp, Spark, TONEX One, Quad Cortex mini, plus "first pedalboard" and "first tube amp" variants. Title pattern targets the exact query wave ("just got", "how to set up", "[gear] for beginners settings").
- Onboarding email capture on those pages: "Day-one tones in your inbox" 5-email sequence, ending at the 5-download cap and the Pass offer.
- Site banner Dec 25 - Jan 15: "New gear under the tree? Start here."
- **KPI:** signups Dec 25 - Jan 15, first paid conversions from the sequence.

### C3: NAMM (late January 2027)

## Standing rules for every seasonal campaign

1. **No fake scarcity, no invented urgency.** Honest windows with real dates. It is the brand.
2. **Gift/buying content is buyer-facing; recipe content is player-facing.** Don't mix registers in one page.
3. **Seasonal pages are evergreen URLs with updated years** (playbook rule), so authority compounds annually: /gift-guide-guitarists, not /gift-guide-2026.
4. **Every seasonal page ends at a recipe, a preset, or an email capture.** Traffic that leaves without a hook is wasted spend of our only scarce resource (attention).
5. **Post-purchase beats pre-purchase for us.** When in doubt, put effort into the unwrapping windows (Dec 25-Jan 15, post-Father's Day, post-refund). Retailers own the transaction; we own the first 30 days of ownership.
6. **The calendar automates.** Each campaign's rank-layer content goes into the normal daily content pipeline as scheduled backlog items 8 weeks ahead; a monthly "seasonal lookahead" scheduled task drafts the campaign assets (see `DISTRIBUTION_AUTOMATION_OS.md`). Human actions per campaign should fit in one 20-minute approval window.
